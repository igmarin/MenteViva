## 1. Demographics: the 60+ wave (WHO, primary)

- **1.1B** people worldwide were 60+ in **2023**; **2.1B by 2050** (~1 in 5
  people). By the late 2060s, 2.5B — surpassing the under-18 population.
  — WHO, *Mental health of older adults*, 2026-07-20
  <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/mental-health-of-older-adults>
- By **2030, 1 in 6** people globally will be 60+ (1.0B in 2020 → 1.4B in
  2030). 80+ population triples 2020→2050 to **426M**.
  — WHO, *Ageing and health*, 2025-10-01
  <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/ageing-and-health>

**So what:** the S1 segment is the largest and fastest-growing consumer
demographic on earth. It is not a niche.

## 2. Demand: 50+ already play games (AARP, primary)

- **52.7M Americans 50+ play video games** — **45% of the 50+ population**
  — AARP, *Gaming Attitudes and Habits of Adults 50-Plus*, June 2023
  (headline: <https://press.aarp.org/2023-6-5-AARP-52-7-Million-Americans-Age-50-Play-Video-Games>;
  summary: <https://www.aarp.org/entertainment/technology/info-2023/adults-age-50-plus-play-video-games.html>)
- AARP operates its own games portal and "Staying Sharp" brain-health program
  for 100M+ Americans 50+ — demand signal plus a potential (long-shot)
  distribution channel. [UNVERIFIED: partnership feasibility never explored]

**So what:** the "seniors don't play mobile games" objection is dead. Nearly
half already do; the failure mode is games that feel childish, punishing, or
confusing — which matches the informal playtest findings (confusing scoring,
boredom, unattractive visuals) posted on issue #6.

## 5. Segment S2: mental-health-managing users (WHO primary + vendor)

- **~15% of adults 70+ live with a mental disorder**; these account for 6.8%
  of years lived with disability in that age group. **Loneliness and social
  isolation are key risk factors** for mental health conditions in later life.
  — WHO, *Mental health of older adults*, 2026-07-20 (same URL as §1)
- Mental-health apps market: **$17.52B by 2030** — Grand View Research press
  release <https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-mental-health-apps-market>
  (directional only; base-year figure behind paywall).
- WHO, July 2026: **up to 45% of dementia risk could be prevented or delayed**
  via modifiable risk factors — WHO News, 2026-07-15. ⚠️ Risk-factor framing
  about lifestyle, **not** about games. Never cite in product copy; see
  `research-2026-09.md` §1 (Lumosity's $2M FTC settlement is the cautionary
  tale for the whole category).

**Regulatory line, made concrete:** Lumosity got FDA 510(k) clearance for
LumosityRx, a *prescription* digital therapeutic for adult ADHD (Dec 2025,
<https://www.lumosity.com/en/press/>). That is what crossing the line looks
like: clinical claims = medical device = FDA. MenteViva stays on the wellness
side — "fun mental exercise" — which also means S2 can only ever be served as
*adjacent* wellness users, never as patients. The copy ceiling is unchanged
and this segment does not change it.

**So what:** S2 is large and overlaps S1 heavily (depression/anxiety in later
life). But it adds regulatory gravity and zero incremental design freedom.
Serve it only through the same warm, non-clinical product — no claims, no
assessments, no tracking-that-looks-clinical.

## 6. Segment S3: general wellness users (weakest case)

- Meditation/wellness apps are a mature, crowded category (Calm, Headspace et
  al.); no primary-source demand stat gathered for "wellness users who want
  casual cognitive games." [UNVERIFIED]
- Lumosity/Elevate already monetize this segment at scale with far bigger
  content libraries (§4).

**So what:** S3 is real but undifferentiated — we have no edge there beyond
price (one-time vs subscription) and bilingual EN+ES. Likely ranking for
Jev 009: S1 ≫ S2 (adjacent) > S3.

## 7. Platform reality check — closes the research half of #41

- Apple official, measured **2026-06-07**: iOS 26 on **79% of all iPhones**
  (86% of devices <4 years old); iPadOS 26 on **68% of all iPads** (79% of
  recent). <https://developer.apple.com/support/app-store/>
- Apple renumbered 18 → 26 in 2025, so "everything else" ≈ iOS 18 + iOS ≤17.
  Mixpanel via Daring Fireball (Aug 2025): iOS 26 60% / iOS 18 30% /
  **iOS 17 ~5.5%**. Trend ⇒ iOS ≤17 is now low single digits and shrinking;
  **iOS ≤16** (what our iOS 17 floor excludes) is a subset of that.
  <https://daringfireball.net/2025/08/apples_latest_ios_adoption_numbers>
- Hardware excluded by an iOS 17 floor: iPhone 8/8 Plus/X (2017) and older;
  iPadOS 17 drops iPad 5th gen (2017) and 1st-gen iPad Pros — 8+-year-old
  devices. Seniors hold devices longer, so their excluded share is a multiple
  of the global ~3–4% — worst case still single digits.

**So what:** the iOS 17 floor costs us low-single-digit percent of the senior
installed base, falling every quarter. Decision stands. Track B closes #41
formally with this data.
