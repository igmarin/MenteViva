MenteViva Redesign

The app is easy to use and kind to its player. It also looks like a settings screen. Every surface is the same pale system blue, the game icons are mismatched emoji, and nothing builds up as your dad plays. The proposal is a card table: each game becomes a printed card in its own colour, Home deals today's three, and every finished game adds a card to his week.

  • Ease of useStrong base100pt targets, one tap into any game, warm copy, no scores.
  • Visual qualityGenericSystem defaults on every screen. No colour, type or shape of its own.
  • AppealLowNothing to look forward to, and nothing that grows as he plays.

What makes it feel generic

Five problems, worst first

  1. There is no visual identity inside the app

    What happens

    Every tile, button and answer is .blue.opacity(0.12–0.25) on white, with green or grey for variety. There is no accent colour asset and no font design. The icon has a palette, but none of it appears once the app opens.

    Fix

    One token set taken from the icon: table gradient, paper, ink, teal, amber, plus one ink per game. System fonts only: New York for greetings and titles, SF Pro Rounded for everything else.

    HomeView.swift:51, :135 · BigTargets.swift:6 · OnboardingView.swift:48, :74 · CelebrationView.swift:39, :49 · CategoryFluencyView.swift:99 · MemoryMatchView.swift:142

  2. Home asks him to choose, and “Today's pick” marks half the grid

    What happens

    Six equal tiles, three of them starred. When half the options are recommended, the star stops meaning anything, and he still has to decide where to start.

    Fix

    Deal today's three as cards and give one 100pt button: “Play today's three”. It runs them back to back. All six games stay one scroll below.

    HomeView.swift:31–44 · DailyPolicy.swift already returns the ordered games

  3. The game icons are five art styles, and two of them read as childish

    What happens

    🧩 is glossy 3D, 🐾 is flat, 🔢 is a keycap, 👀 is a cartoon, 📷 is photo-real. Playtest #109 already said “a game, but he's not a child”. The cartoon eyes and paw prints work against that.

    Fix

    Six emblems drawn in one flat, printed-card style, each in its game's ink. Interim: SF Symbols in the same coloured chips.

    Models/GameID.swift:14–19

  4. Game screens give more room to chrome than to the task

    What happens

    A large navigation title takes the top fifth, the task floats mid-screen, and the only orientation cue, “1 / 10”, is secondary grey at 3.4:1. That passes the large-text minimum, but it is the hardest text on screen for older eyes.

    Fix

    A compact shared header: back, game name, a read-aloud button. Progress becomes ten small cards plus “Round 4 of 10” in full-contrast ink. Answers sit low, in thumb reach.

    RoundCounter.swift:9–10 · .navigationTitle in each Games/*View.swift

  5. The reward is the same every time, and nothing adds up

    What happens

    Every game ends with the same confetti and “Well done! 🎉”. Play again is blue and Home is green, but the colours carry no meaning. My Week is a list, or a sunflower when empty. There is nothing he can watch grow.

    Fix

    Each finished game adds its card to the week. The celebration shows the card landing on today; My Week shows the week as a table of cards. Still no scores.

    CelebrationView.swift · HistoryView.swift

Apple Design Award winners, 2024–2026

What the winners do that MenteViva doesn't yet

Used as reference for principles only. Nothing here copies their visuals.

NYT Games

2024 winner · Delight and Fun
What it does
One hub of daily puzzles. Each game owns a colour and a simple emblem, and today's puzzles lead the screen.
Take for MenteViva
A colour and emblem per game, and a Home that opens on today.

Balatro

2025 winner · Delight and Fun
What it does
Cards feel like objects: they deal, tilt, flip and settle with weight.
Take for MenteViva
Cards are already in the icon and in Memory Lane. Make them the whole app's material.

Art of Fauna

2025 winner · Inclusivity
What it does
Vintage-style illustration, puzzles that pair pictures with text, full VoiceOver and haptics.
Take for MenteViva
Grown-up illustration and strong accessibility fit together. This answers “he's not a child”.

Gentler Streak

2024 winner · Social Impact
What it does
An encouraging tone, and progress measured against yourself, never against others.
Take for MenteViva
Your copy already does this. The week of cards makes it visible.

Crouton

2024 winner · Interaction
What it does
Uncluttered screens that keep attention on the task in front of you.
Take for MenteViva
In a game, remove the large title and anything that isn't the task.

Guitar Wiz

2026 winner · Inclusivity
What it does
Spoken guidance, with Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast and Differentiate Without Colour.
Take for MenteViva
“Read names aloud” is buried in Settings. Put a speaker button on every instruction.

If you pick Direction A

Build order, smallest visible win first

Before step 1, about 30 minutes: show your dad the three Home screens above on a phone and note which one he reaches for. Then log the choice in docs/decisions.md with a Jev judgment, as AGENTS.md asks for product decisions.

  1. Tokens and fonts

    ½ day

    Colour assets with light and dark values, .fontDesign(.rounded) at the root, serif on headings. Replace the nine tint call sites. The whole app changes character in one PR.

For the next round

Four decisions that shape the next iteration

  • Which world feels like your dad's game?Card Table, Garden or Calm Glass.
  • Should today's three run back to back?Or return to the table between games.
  • Simple emblems or commissioned art?Emblems ship this week; illustration takes 1–2 weeks and a budget.
