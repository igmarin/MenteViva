MenteViva · Card Table, round 3

Privacy he can read, and an icon that matches the cards

Two things you flagged: the privacy text in Ajustes, and the app's logo. For each, what he sees now, what's wrong with it, and what I'd change. Everything stays offline and needs no new libraries or images: the icon is still drawn by tools/generate_icon.swift.

1 · Privacy

Three promises instead of one paragraph

What's wrong now
  • One paragraph holds six separate promises. At his text size it's a wall of grey, and the reassuring part ("everything stays here") is buried in the middle.
  • It talks about "sugerencias diarias", a setting he can't see. His build has no proxy configured, so nothing at all leaves the iPad, and that sentence only plants a doubt.
  • It sits at the very bottom, under Mi progreso and Ajustes de juego, in .body size. Everywhere else the app uses .title3 and up.
  • It still says "añadas", the Spain form. The rest of the app now uses "agregar".
Why this version
  • Three lines he can read in five seconds, each true for his iPad. The device name comes from the system, so on the owner's phone it says "este iPhone".
  • The "anonymous totals" line only appears in builds where daily suggestions really are on (proxy URL set). The text then matches what the app does.
  • The backup line stays, in smaller type, because it's true and the policy needs it. It just isn't a headline.
  • A teal frame like the game cards. No lock or shield icon: those read as "security warning" to many older people.
Two smaller places, same words

Welcome screen: one line under the language choice, "Sin cuenta. Todo se queda en este iPad." That way the first thing he learns is that nothing needs signing up. Photo name sheet: keep the existing line; it already says exactly what happens to a name he types.

English: "Your games stay on this iPad." · "No account, no ads, no tracking." · "Your photos and their names never leave it." · "If you back up this iPad, the backup keeps them too."

2 · The logo

The icon still shows the old cards

Inside the app every game is now a framed card: white paper, a frame in the game's ink, and a drawn emblem. The icon is two blank cream cards with a dot on a softer gradient, so on his home screen it looks like a different app from the one that opens. The Memory Lane emblem already is the logo, drawn in the new colours: a pale card behind a teal card with the amber dot.

Now current

Blank cards, no frame. At 60 points the dot is the only detail you can see.

A · The framed card recommended

One game card, exactly as Home deals them, on the table colours. It reads at 60 points: a white card, a teal frame, an amber dot. It's also what the end of each game shows, so the icon and the reward are the same object.

B · The hand

Today's three, fanned like on Home. Warm and very "card table", but at 60 points the side cards turn into colour smudges.

C · Same cards, new colours

The smallest change: today's shape, recoloured to the Memory Lane emblem and the table gradient. It matches, but it's the emblem, not a card, so it doesn't echo the framed faces.

The name at the top of Home

Home's top bar says "Mente Viva" in small plain text: the one place the app names itself, and the least designed thing on the screen. I'd swap it for the icon's card as a small mark beside the name in the same serif as "Buenos días".
