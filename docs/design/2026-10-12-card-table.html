Card Table

You picked the Card Table. This round defines the card itself, gives Home a state for every part of the day, lays out iPad, and seats all six games at the table. It ends with the SwiftUI pieces, the new EN + ES strings, and a build order.

Defaults I chose
  • Framed card facesThe name sits on a band in the game's colour. Two other faces are below if you prefer one.
  • Today's three run back to back“Back to the table” stays one tap away at every step.
  • Emblems drawn in SwiftUIPlain shapes, so no illustrator or image files are needed to ship.
Fixed since round 1
  • Name plates are easier to readWas 15pt coloured text on white. Now 16pt on a solid band, 5.4–8.6:1.
  • My Week has a door on HomeA week board sits right under today's table.
  • Home knows when he's doneThe cards stack up and the week fills in.
  • Name the Herd's emblem makes senseThree in the pen, one left out.
1 · The card

One object carries every game

The same card appears on Home, at the end of a game and in My Week. Only its size changes.

4 · Every game at the table

Six games, one header, the task in front

Every game uses the same header: back, the game's name, and a speaker that reads the instruction aloud. Progress is a row of small cards in the game's ink, with words at full contrast. Answers sit low, within thumb reach.

6 · Build spec

Seven pieces, no dependencies

SwiftUI and colour assets only, per conventions §1. Each piece replaces something that exists today.

PieceWhat it isReplaces
ThemeColour assets with light and dark values; .fontDesign(.rounded) at the root, .serif on greetings and titlesThe nine pale blue, green and grey tints; default fonts
EmblemSix small SwiftUI shape drawings, one per gameGameID.emoji
GameCardOne view, four sizes: hand, big, chip, week. States: ready, pressed, played todayGameTile in HomeView
TableBackgroundThe icon gradient, light and darkPlain background
GameHeaderBack, game name, read-aloud via CaptionSpeaker, progress cards with RoundCounter's VoiceOver label.navigationTitle, RoundCounter
TodayRunPure logic: which of today's games comes next and when the day is done. Tested first, Given/When/ThenNothing (new)

Build order

  1. Theme

    ½ day

    Colour assets, root font design, swap the nine tints. Every screen changes character in one small PR.

  2. Emblem and GameCard

    1 day

    GameID gains an ink and an emblem. Home's tiles become cards.

  3. GameHeader in all six games

    1 day

    Large titles go; the read-aloud button and progress cards arrive. The amber “Almost” note goes in here too.

  4. Home: table, TodayRun, iPad

    2 days

    Three states, the week board, and the two-column iPad layout from horizontalSizeClass. TodayRun tests come first. Run tools/generate_project.swift for the new files.

  5. End of game and My Week

    2 days

    Card lands on today, Next button during today's three, WeekBoard on top of My Week. The share text stays as it is.

About 6½ working days in total. Not covered yet: Settings and For family. They keep the system list style and only pick up the theme.
