Support

The app stopped opening after a week (beta installs)

Free-account test builds expire 7 days after they were installed. Whoever installed it for you can refresh it in two minutes — the device just needs to visit the Mac it came from. Your games and progress are kept.

Switching language

Open Settings inside the app and pick English or Español. Everything switches, including the games.

Where is my progress?

On your device, only. There is no account and nothing to sign into — if the app is deleted, its stored sessions go with it.

Something doesn't look right

Write to hello@nebulaideas.com — tell us the device model and what you saw (a photo of the screen helps a lot). We answer in English or Spanish.
